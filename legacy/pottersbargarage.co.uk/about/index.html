ABOUT

We Care About More Than Car

Potters Bar Garage offers a comprehensive range of services for all types of cars and motorbikes. Our aim is to provide a high-quality service at affordable prices. We welcome both private customers and fleet work. We are able to provide same day servicing on all makes of private and light commercial vehicles for your convenience.
We provide comprehensive repair services under one roof– whether you need Tyre replacement or MOT testing, servicing, diagnostic, air-conditioning service, bodywork or spare parts replacement, our team of experts are here to provide fair and honest advise. At Potters Bar garage, our expert technicians provide all the major system checks and adjustments for all makes and models. Furthermore, we take pride in all our work that we do, regardless of the job whether it is big or small. Similarly, we only use reputable brands who produce excellent quality parts suitable for your vehicle to maximize the reliability and usability of your vehicle. Our work is fully guaranteed for one year, this means that if anything goes wrong, we have fitted or with the work we have completed we will take full responsibility.
